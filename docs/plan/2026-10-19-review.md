## 2. Decided, not reopened

Each decision stands. Evidence against any of them is listed with its source,
and only where the evidence is hard.

| Decision | Evidence against | Source | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pricing**: free for every sender, enterprise per card | The repo's own research of 12 Oct argues for £9.99 a month to trades, needing about 300 paying trades for a £36k run rate, and says there is no UK data on whether a sole trader will pay for this as a separate line at all. "Test £9.99 on ten real trades before building any billing." It also says the lifetime value at that price rules out any paid acquisition, which is the case for the ServiceM8 store as the only channel, free or not. | `docs/research/shop-competitive-landscape.md`, "Price it at £9.99 a month" and "The arithmetic, stated bluntly" | **Hard, and already on the plan as a risk** ("Free for everyone earns nothing for a year", high). Nothing to decide tonight. Worth adding the research's one cheap test to move 3: ask ten ServiceM8 trades, once, whether they would pay £9.99. It costs nothing and answers the only unknown that matters. |
| **First wedge**: social housing, "no incumbent tracker" | Fortem, a housing repairs contractor, offers "On My Way" tracking to residents: a day-before text and a live ETA on the day. So there is an incumbent at some landlords' contractors. The rest of the decision note holds: a repairs manager you can meet, a tenancy as an account per address. | `docs/research/inpost-and-the-day-of-service.md`, the field table and the Fortem link (housing-technology.com) | **Hard, narrow**.
