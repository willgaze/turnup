## Headline

The customer's job link is real and almost everything the roadmap calls "Working
now" is in the code and wired to a page: stages, windows that are agreed rather
than announced, the day-before ask, still coming?, not home, pause words,
revised hours, offline taps, ServiceM8 sync, the review row. Live checks agree:
`/api/status/ZZZZZZZZ` answers 404 (database healthy), push is configured (the
notifications row renders on /demo), ServiceM8 is connected (/with-servicem8
says so), the letterbox is receiving (`/api/places` → `receiving: true`),
what3words conversion is off (`/api/w3w` → `configured: false`). What is not
live: the wallet pass (unsigned, and it would not update itself even once
signed, because `/api/passes/v1` does not exist), the iOS app, the van lookup (but see the correction below)
(keys not set), the ServiceM8 diary add-on, and everything under "Next" and
"The bigger idea" bar the household card, which is live but missing from the
roadmap. One real defect on the customer's page: "Are you still coming?" breaks
React's hook order and will crash the page if it is open when the window starts.
The pilot panel counts, but "links opened" includes the trade's own opens and
link-preview fetches, and the thirty days roll rather than start on a date.

## Pilot numbers

`node scripts/check-pilot.mjs`: all 8 checks pass ("The pilot numbers hold."). The
checks test the arithmetic on a fixture, not what the rows mean.

Window for all four: rolling, `now − 30 days` to `now` (or 90), by `scheduledFor`,
else `createdAt` (src/lib/pilot.js:24-32). Revoked cards (`isActive: false`) are
left out. There is no pilot start date anywhere, so today it reaches back to
18 Sep and takes in every test card made since launch. The fixture card
K7M4PQRT is not in production (live /api/status/K7M4PQRT → 404) and is not
counted; test jobs made by following /with-servicem8 "Make a test job for
yourself" are, and nothing marks a card as a test.

| Number | Counted from | Right? | Problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| Links opened | `TradeStatus.viewCount > 0`, per job, over jobs in the window (pilot.js:36) | No, too high | viewCount goes up on every load of `/t/<code>` by anyone (src/app/t/[code]/page.js:38): the trade's own "Open" (Console.jsx:702, Sm8Week.jsx:104), link-preview fetchers (WhatsApp, iMessage, mail link scanners; likely, not tested), and the trade's own test claims. One such load makes an unopened job "opened". Repeat opens do not inflate it (it is >0). Worse: with Follow every booking off, a ServiceM8 card only exists once the customer opens `/j/` (src/app/j/[...ref]/page.js:76-77), so opened is near 100% by construction; unopened links never become a row |
| "Will someone be in?" answered | `presenceAt` set, over ALL jobs in the window (pilot.js:38) | Partly | Counts answers given at any time, not replies to the ask; "need to move it" does not count (nudge.js:246); clearing an answer removes it (notes route:60). The % is of all jobs, including those never asked (same-day bookings, cards with no day: day-before.js:44-46); the tile shows "asked on N" but not answered ÷ asked |
| Wasted trips | `NoAccess` rows whose `startedAt` is in the window AND whose job is in the window (pilot.js:39) | No, too low (and sometimes double) | A missed job that is rebooked moves `scheduledFor` into the future, drops out of the window, and takes its No one home record with it. A revoked card does the same. A record is per tap, not per visit; an online No one home that times out at 12 s is queued and replayed under a new tapId, giving two rows (offline-queue.js:65-78). Only trips the trade records exist |
| "When are you coming?" calls | Nothing: `calls.n` is always null (pilot.js:51). Beside it, jobs with `askedAt` set (pilot.js:40) | Honest, but unusable | The panel says count by hand and offers no place to put the count, so it cannot sit beside the other three for thirty days.
