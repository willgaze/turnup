# TurnUp privacy policy — DRAFT, not published

*Written 17 Oct 2026 from what the code and the database actually hold, for
the ServiceM8 Add-on Store's "Privacy Policy" field. It is a draft because
three things in it are the owner's to decide, marked **DECIDE**. Nothing here
is live until it is agreed and put on a page at getturnup.com/privacy.*

---

## Who runs TurnUp

TurnUp is run by **DECIDE: the legal name TurnUp trades under, and an address
for notices**. Questions about this policy go to **DECIDE: a monitored email
address** (getturnup.com has no mailbox today).

## Who this covers

- **Trades** who use TurnUp to send their customers a link to a job.
- **Their customers**, who open that link. A customer never makes an account.
- **Households** given a household card, and anyone who forwards an email to it.
- **People on the waiting list** on the homepage.

## What TurnUp holds, and why

| Whose | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The trade's customer, from the trade | Name, mobile, address, what the job is, the day and any hours agreed, the job reference | So the trade can send one link that says where the job is |
| The customer, from the customer | Which door, whether there is a dog, access notes, a map pin or what3words, whether someone will be in, replies to the day-before question, a private note after the job | So the trade can find the door and does not waste a trip. **Never shown on the link**, which can be forwarded; only the trade sees it, behind a password |
| The trade, about a visit | When each stage was tapped, the location fix taken when "no one home" is recorded, a photo of the door, attempts made | A record of the visit, kept by the trade |
| The trade | Name, van, plate, photo, logo, booking terms, a Google review link | Shown to the customer from the moment the trade sets off |
| A household card | The carrier, a title, a reference and the date or hours the sender gave, read from forwarded emails. The email itself is not kept | So one page shows everything coming to that address |
| A phone that asked for notifications | The browser's push address | To send the stage to that phone, and nothing else |
| The waiting list | Email, trade, town | To say when there is room |

## Who else it passes through

| Service | What it sees | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Vercel | Everything above, as the host | Runs the site |
| Neon | Everything above, as the database | Stores it |
| ServiceM8 | Reads the trade's own jobs; writes lines to that job's diary when the add-on is connected | The trade's own job system |
| Cloudflare | Forwarded emails, in transit | Delivers mail to a household card |
| Apple, Google, Mozilla push services | The stage and the trade's name, nothing about the customer | Delivers a notification |
| Anthropic | The trade's own job list, **without phone numbers**, when the trade uses the Ask box | Answers the trade's question |
| DVSA | A number plate | Fills in the van's make and colour |

TurnUp does not sell data, show adverts, or use it to train anything.

## How long it is kept

**DECIDE.** Today nothing is deleted on a timetable: a job's record stays until
it is removed. A trade can switch any link off, after which it shows nothing.
A suggestion to agree: job records kept for six years (the period a trade keeps
job records for tax), customer-supplied access notes and photos deleted ninety
days after Job done, waiting-list entries deleted on request.

## Your rights

Anyone can ask what is held about them, have it corrected, or have it deleted,
by writing to the address above. A customer's request about a job goes to the
trade who booked it as well, because it is their record of their job. You can
also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## What TurnUp will never do

- Put a customer's answers on a link that can be forwarded.
- Tell anyone a time nobody agreed.
- Take a payment.
